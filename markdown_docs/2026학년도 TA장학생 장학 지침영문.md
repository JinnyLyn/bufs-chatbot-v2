1. TA Scholarship Program Guide 

- A. Scholarship Name: TA Scholarship 

- B. Relevant Regulations 

Scholarship Payment Enforcement Rules, Article 2, Clause 73 – TA Scholarship: 

A certain amount of scholarship may be awarded to students who support educational activities. (Established on December 27, 2022) 

- C. Purpose of Operation 

   - 1) Management of newly admitted students through integrated recruitment 

- 2) Support for faculty members’ student guidance activities and tutoring (teaching assistant duties) 

- 3) Support for freshmen’s adaptation to university life (prevention of student dropouts) 

- 4) Expansion of scholarship opportunities 

2. Operation Method 

- A. Operating Period 

- 1) First Semester : March 3, 2026 (Tue) – June 12, 2026 (Fri) 

- 2) Second Semester : August 31, 2026 (Mon) – December 11, 2026 (Fri) 

- B. TA Student Duties 

- 1) Assistance with faculty members’ student support and educational activities 

- Support for student guidance and academic assistance activities of the supervising professor 

- Assistance in classes taught by foreign faculty members 

- 2) Support for curricular and extracurricular class operation 

- Educational support personnel: Perform the role of a class teaching assistant by supporting overall class operation, including preparing class notices and materials, and assisting with experiment-, practice-, performance-, design-, and activity-based classes 

- HyFlex class support: Attendance monitoring for online sections, technical support for class operation, and tutoring activities 

- 3) Support for departmental operation and student activities 

- Freshman management: grievance counseling, operation of study groups, major promotion, etc. 

- Department homepage and SNS management: posting notices, uploading promotional photos and videos, etc. 

- Support for department club operation and activities 

- Support for management of laboratories and practice rooms 

- 4) Other tasks that contribute to improving TA students’ social adaptability and employability or serve the public interest 

- ※ Tasks not eligible for TA assignment 

- Tasks that cannot be managed by the college/department 

- Other inappropriate tasks 

- C. Operating Procedures 

- 1) One TA scholarship student shall be assigned per one full-time faculty member 

--- end of page.page_number=2 ---

- 2) TA students are selected through the supervising professor’s recommendation, after which the college/department submits an official request to the Student Welfare Team 

- 3) Ongoing announcements, TA workshops, and mid-semester meetings for interim evaluation will be conducted 

- 4) Upon completion of the operation period, students and supervising professors must submit activity opinion forms and activity confirmation certificates via official documents 

- Failure to submit required documents within the designated period will result in ineligibility for scholarship payment 

- 5) Scholarship payment to selected students (KRW 1,000,000 per semester) 

- D. Operating Method 

- 1) During the operating period, working hours and work format are managed by the professor and operated autonomously. 

- The total activity hours for the semester should not exceed 96 hours. 

- 2) The operation should be conducted smoothly with the mutual agreement between the professor and the student, ensuring that it does not interfere with the student's classes. 

- E. Selection Criteria 

- 1) Professors affiliated with a department can select and recommend students from their own 

   - department. 

- Full-time professors affiliated with administrative departments can select students 

   - regardless of the department. 

- 2) Students in their second year or above may apply (newly admitted and transfer students of 

   - the same year are excluded) 

- Students in excess semesters, students who have been expelled, graduates, graduate students, students on leave of absence, and students under academic suspension cannot apply. 

- If there is any change in student status during the semester, the scholarship cannot be awarded. 

- For newly established departments in 2026, first-year students are eligible. 

- 3) Students who have been selected as TA scholarship recipients for two or more semesters are not eligible. 

- 4) It is recommended that professors teaching courses with a high proportion of international students, as well as foreign full-time professors, select international students for the scholarship. 

- Only students who were newly admitted or transferred to the university are eligible. 

- Exchange students are not eligible. 

- F. Important Notes 

- 1) The scholarship period is for one semester, but students can reapply in the following semester (up to two times). 

- 2) Duplicate selection is not allowed
